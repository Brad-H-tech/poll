    Why this is not optional

    Clause 31 of your employment contract requires you to follow the practices and procedures the network prescribes, in support of the Dealer Agreement Cell Logic holds with the network. Failing to do so — particularly on administrative records — can result in disciplinary charges and, if found guilty, dismissal.

    Before this goes to the floor

    This matrix was rebuilt from a scanned copy of the 09 September 2025 notice. A few cells on that scan were hard to read with confidence and should be confirmed against the original before this is issued to staff:

    • Proof of residence and driver's licence rows on the New Month-2-Month column
    • Whether Latest payslip or Payslip not older than 3 months applies on each EC column
    • The self-employed column: the notice shows bank statements rather than a payslip, which is worth restating explicitly
