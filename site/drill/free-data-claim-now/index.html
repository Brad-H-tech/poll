You just clicked a sketchy link. Don't worry, this one was us. A real scammer wouldn't be so friendly. 😉

That's how easy it is. So let's advise our clients not to click sketchy links: "free data", "you've won", "your account is suspended", "confirm your SIM swap"…

And this is exactly why dealing with Cell Logic is safer. We do it face to face, in store, with real people, and we never ask for PINs or OTPs through a link.

Before you click, check:

  • 🔗 The address. Weird spelling, odd endings, or shortened links? Don't click.
  • ⏰ The pressure. "Claim NOW", "expires today" is a scam's favourite trick.
  • 🎁 Too good to be true? Free iPhones and free data don't come by SMS.
  • 🔐 PINs, passwords, OTPs. Never share them. MTN and Cell Logic will never ask.
  • ❓ Not sure? Ask a manager, or call the company on a number you already trust.

Relax: this page collects nothing. No passwords, no data, no tracking. It was just a lesson. 😏
